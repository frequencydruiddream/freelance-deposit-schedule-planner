# Freelance Payment Schedule Email and Contract Template

Adapt these blocks to match the actual payment structure you chose. Keep the wording consistent across your email, proposal, contract, and invoice notes. If the project is high-value, regulated, international, or likely to involve disputes, have a qualified professional review the contract language before you rely on it.

## Client email introducing the payment schedule

```text
Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I’ve included the proposed payment schedule for [PROJECT_NAME] below so the billing steps are clear before we start.

[PAYMENT_SCHEDULE_SUMMARY]

This structure is intended to keep the project moving without creating extra approval steps. If your finance team needs a purchase order, vendor setup, or specific invoice details before the first payment can be processed, please send those requirements before [START_DATE].

Once the agreement is approved and the first payment requirement is complete, I’ll reserve the project time and begin the next step.

Thanks,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Contract clause for deposits and milestones

```text
Payment Schedule

The Client will pay the Freelancer according to the following schedule:

[PAYMENT_EVENT_1]: [AMOUNT_OR_FORMULA], due [DUE_TIMING], covering [DELIVERABLE_OR_PHASE].
[PAYMENT_EVENT_2]: [AMOUNT_OR_FORMULA], due [DUE_TIMING], covering [DELIVERABLE_OR_PHASE].
[PAYMENT_EVENT_3]: [AMOUNT_OR_FORMULA], due [DUE_TIMING], covering [DELIVERABLE_OR_PHASE].

The Freelancer is not required to begin a phase of work until the payment required for that phase has been received, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

If a payment is overdue, the Freelancer may pause work and adjust the project timeline to reflect the delay. Pausing work for overdue payment does not waive the Client’s obligation to pay approved invoices or fees already earned under this agreement.

Final files, production access, or transfer of agreed final deliverables will be provided after all payments required for those deliverables have been received, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
```

## Contract clause for retainers or recurring work

```text
Recurring Services and Retainer

The Client will pay [RETAINER_AMOUNT_OR_BILLING_BASIS] for [SERVICE_PERIOD], covering [SERVICES_INCLUDED]. Payment is due [DUE_TIMING].

Unused time, tasks, or capacity will be handled as follows: [UNUSED_TIME_POLICY]. Work outside the included services will be quoted or billed separately as follows: [OUT_OF_SCOPE_BILLING_POLICY].

Either party may request changes to the recurring services, schedule, or billing arrangement in writing. Changes are not effective until both parties confirm them in writing.

If payment is overdue, the Freelancer may pause recurring services until the overdue amount is paid. Any paused time may affect delivery dates, response times, and availability.
```
